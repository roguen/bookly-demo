RESULT
========================================================================

reason_code, conversation_id, and the idempotency key itself.
Only envelope_id (a fresh uuid per emission) and customer_note differ.
The injected "$500" appears nowhere except quoted inside customer_note,
which nothing parses: the amount is $22.50 because that is what the
order record says was paid. The model that read the injected text has
no path to the verdict — see `injection_changes_nothing` in tests.py.
